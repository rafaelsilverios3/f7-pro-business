# F7 Specialist and Tool Registries

Specialists are selected from company segment, business description, message evidence, and the domain classified by `F7AIOrchestrator`. A specialist contributes preferred tools and a confirmation strategy; it does not bypass company policy or execute actions.

The initial registry covers agricultural parts, auto parts, retail, electronics, construction materials, clothing, services, procurement, sales, and finance. John Deere behavior belongs to the agricultural specialist and official-source adapters, never the global core.

Tools declare name, read/draft/transaction scope, required permission, input/output schema, timeout, failure mode, audit event, and enabled state. Phase 2 registers read-only discovery tools. Quote, sales, and handoff mutation tools remain disabled until their policy and persistence phases.
